About

Ümit Sural is a Barcelona / Paris based multidisciplinary artist.

His practice moves between figuration and abstraction, often focusing on the tension between presence and absence, visibility and withdrawal. Working across painting, sculpture and image, Sural approaches each series as a distinct inquiry while maintaining a reduced and psychologically charged visual language. Read more →
